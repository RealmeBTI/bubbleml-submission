# Author Biography

**S. B. Mahafuj Bondhon** is a mechanical engineering researcher at the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). His research focuses on Scientific Machine Learning (SciML), operator learning (Fourier neural operators), and physics-informed neural surrogates for chaotic, multiphase transport phenomena. His work emphasizes reproducible evaluation protocols, strict physical conservation enforcement in neural PDE solvers, and auditable statistical benchmarking for thermal-fluid applications.

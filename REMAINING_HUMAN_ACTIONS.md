# Remaining Human Actions

Only facts or actions requiring the author are listed here.

- Confirm the complete author list and order, affiliations, corresponding author,
  postal address, email, and phone number.
- Confirm CRediT roles. The supplied role table is not treated as verified.
- Confirm the conflict-of-interest statement and replace its bracketed qualifier if needed.
- Provide the exact funding statement and grant identifiers, or explicitly confirm no external funding.
- Choose the final target journal and confirm its live submission requirements,
  peer-review anonymity policy, file types, and graphical-abstract requirement.
- Provide or approve the missing citations listed in `BIBLIOGRAPHY_VERIFICATION.md`.
- Choose a repository code license. No code license is asserted in this package.
- Create/approve the public GitHub repository and URL, push this local repository,
  and make the required checkpoints/results accessible.
- Link the GitHub repository to the author's Zenodo account, publish the final
  release, obtain the DOI, and insert the DOI/URLs in the manuscript and statements.
- Map the three unassigned recorded hashes in `CHECKSUMS.md` to exact filenames.
- Supply or export the complete cross-condition per-seed configs/histories and the
  field-snapshot inputs identified in `ARTIFACT_GAPS.md`.
- Decide whether to post a preprint and, if so, insert its verified URL/status.
- Create the journal submission-system account, enter metadata, select reviewers
  after checking conflicts, upload files, and perform the final submission click.
- Review and approve every final document, including the unchanged statements
  about manuscript approval, exclusivity, conflicts, and ethics.
